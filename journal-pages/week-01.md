## Documentation 

### What did you choose to track, and why?
* I chose to track the following 5 topics.
When I make eye contact with a stranger while walking - I chose this because I thought it would be interesting to see how often we connect with strangers without ever thinking about it.
How many times I cried - I feel that I am a very emotional person, and I wanted to see how many times I cried over a written-down period.
How many times I had a hug - This leads off me being an emotional person who also likes physical contact from my friends and family.
How many times I thought about sewing - I have been really into sewing recently, and it is consuming my thoughts when I am bored or doing something else.
Every time I like something someone else had - I observe things that other people have often, not out of jealousy, but from curiosity and inspiration. Seeing what people choose to wear and buy is very interesting to me, especially when I see choices I might not have thought of.
### What was it like to collect and visualise this data?
* Collecting the data was sometimes challenging, as it was hard to remember when these prompts happened over the four days. The easiest prompt for me to collect was prompt 2, while prompt 1 was the hardest due to the high number of responses. Instead of tallying every instance, I would estimate how many times I made eye contact over a period, such as while walking around the neighbourhood. Visualising the data wasn’t as difficult as I expected. Once I had all my data, I spent time drafting different ways to represent it, which helped me finalise my design.
### What did you notice that you wouldn’t have otherwise?
* I noticed that the day I cried the most was also the day I received the most hugs. This could have been due to friends and family comforting me. It’s something I would be interested in exploring further, as over a longer period, it might show a stronger relationship. However, since I collected data over only 4 days, it’s hard to confirm a clear correlation.
### What choices did you make for your data collection? What does it emphasise? What is left out?
* For prompt 1, I only collected data when I made eye contact with strangers while travelling from point A to point B, for example, walking through the shopping centre or going to the gym. I did not include people I interacted with, such as shop employees or gym members.
### How does this exercise relate to data humanism and the ‘Dear Data’ project?
* This exercise aligns with data humanism and 'Dear Data' by turning small, personal moments into meaningful visual stories rather than just numbers. My project focuses on subjective and emotional experiences, such as crying, hugging, and noticing others, which are not usually captured in traditional data sets. The imperfections in my data, like estimating eye contact, reflect the human aspect of data and show that it doesn’t need to be perfectly precise to be valuable. By hand-drawing my visualisation and creating my own visual language, I was able to express personality and individuality, similar to ‘Dear Data’. Overall, this project shows how data can be intimate, reflective, and expressive, helping to tell a deeper story about everyday life.
