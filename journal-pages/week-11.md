## - In Class Work 

### 1. Journal Review

*Instead of swapping journals with a partner, I decided to do a personal review of my own work. I went through my journal using the checklist as a guide, flagging anything that was missing, incomplete, or not up to the standard I wanted. I made notes on each section, so I had a clear picture of what still needed attention, whether that was finishing something off, adding more detail, or just tidying things up.*
*Overall, I don't have too much to catch up on, which is a good sign and means I've been keeping on top of things fairly well throughout the project. That said, going through everything did make me realise that some of my entries are more descriptive than reflective. A lot of them focus on what I did rather than why I made certain choices or what I actually took from the experience. That's the main thing I want to work on before submission: making my reflections more in-depth and more critical.*
*The other thing I want to sort out is the journal's overall presentation. I want it to feel like one cohesive piece of work rather than a bunch of separate entries that don't quite connect. Going back through earlier sections and making sure they're as considered as the later ones will help with that. Heading into the final stretch, I feel good about where I'm at, but I know the quality of my reflection matters just as much as having everything completed, so that's where I'll be putting most of my energy.*
