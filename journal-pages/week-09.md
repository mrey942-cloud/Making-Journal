### 1. Project Statement: First Draft

*Data sources - Labour and productivity metrics pulled from existing government and corporate systems. The kind of data already used to track, quantify, and evaluate human work*
<br>

*Future scenario - Automated systems have taken over the management of human labour entirely. Optimisation and efficiency logic pushed to its absolute extreme*
<br>

*Data and power - Data is never neutral; it always reflects the values of whoever built the system. When systems are designed around productivity and profit, the data serves those interests, not the people being measured*
<br>

*Intended impact - Provocation and discomfort aim to unsettle you by systems that feel familiar. The statement is more descriptive than argumentative, which feels like a deliberate choice. It shows you what the system does rather than telling you what to think.*
<br>

Nothing about the 28-day observation period or how the piece accumulates and changes over time*

Too academic. The second paragraph tries to do too much and loses momentum.*

*What you need to research further- A clearer definition of data feminism, who coined it, what it actually argues, and why it applies here. Examples of other data physicalisation projects to reference as context for your own approach. The near-future app scenario could be more specific. Are there apps like this already emerging? That research would make the speculative angle much sharper.*
