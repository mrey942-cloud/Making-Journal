# Making Journal for DES240

*Hi, my name is Madison Reynolds, and I am an aspiring 3rd-year designer. I haven't found my niche in the design community yet, so I am very open to different design courses offered by the university design committee. I decided to take Design 224: 'Designing with Data,' because the idea of using metrics, user analytics, and research to guide and validate creative choices really intrigued me. This course has developed my critical thinking and reflective skills, which is what I had hoped for. As you will see in this making journal, I have been reflecting on my progress throughout this course and on the making and production of my final artefact, titled 'Friendship Maintenance Over Distance.'*
